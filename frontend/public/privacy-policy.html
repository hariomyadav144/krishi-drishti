Fasal Drishti – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026
Application Name: Fasal Drishti (फ़सल दृष्टि)
Package ID: com.fasaldristi.app

Fasal Drishti ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of farmers, agricultural consultants, and all users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our Android application and associated cloud services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Data (Precise and Approximate): Collected only with your explicit permission when utilizing the GPS Farm Mapping tool to walk field boundaries and calculate acreage, and to deliver hyper-local 5-day weather advisories. Location data is stored securely in your user account to display your mapped fields. We do NOT track your background location when the app is closed.
  • Camera and Photos: Collected only when you capture or upload plant leaf photos for AI crop disease analysis. Images are processed exclusively to diagnose agricultural conditions and recommend organic or chemical remedies.
  • Audio Recordings: Collected temporarily when you use the SWAR voice search feature. Audio is processed ephemerally for speech-to-text conversion and is NOT retained or sold.
  • User Account Information: Name, phone number or email address, and encrypted credentials used solely for authentication and farm profile management.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) satellite insights and field mapping overlays.
  • To detect crop diseases and deliver tailored agronomic advice.
  • To provide local APMC mandi wholesale price trends and precision weather advisories.
  • To maintain user sessions and personalize crop management records.

3. Third-Party Sharing and Data Sale

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or agricultural data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure providers (such as encrypted cloud hosting and satellite open data feeds) strictly necessary to deliver the application's core functionality.

4. Data Security

All network communications between the Fasal Drishti Android app and our backend servers are secured using industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.2/1.3). Passwords and sensitive credentials are encrypted using salted cryptographic hashes (bcrypt).

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Users may request the deletion of their account, farm boundaries, and diagnostic history at any time by contacting our support team at yadavhariom8819@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all associated user data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Fasal Drishti is designed for adult agricultural professionals and farmers (ages 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
